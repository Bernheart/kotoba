# Kanji: Polish meanings to review

The kanji data (`lesson-03.json` … `lesson-12.json`) was transcribed from the Genki I textbook pages (printed p. 304 onwards). Genki gives English only, so **every Polish (PL) meaning below was written for this app** and should be checked. Fix a meaning by editing the `pl` field in the lesson file.

Notes on the transcription:

- **々 (133)** has no reading in the book (it is the repetition mark), so its `on` and `kun` are empty and it never gets a reading question.
- Words the book prints with two readings or spellings keep the book’s ／, e.g. 七 しち／なな, 入り口／入口; both are accepted in the quiz.
- Stroke counts were checked against KanjiVG: all 145 match the book.
