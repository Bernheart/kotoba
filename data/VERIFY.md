# Entries to double-check

All vocabulary in this app was written for it (not copied from Genki). These entries carry a note because
the reading, nuance, translation or lesson placement may differ from your course. Fix them in the app
(Words → tap the word → Edit) or in the JSON file, then delete the `verify` field.

**6 entries** across all lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Greetings & new friends

- **情報学** (じょうほうがく) — informatics; computer science / informatyka  
  Universities also say 情報科学 or コンピューターサイエンス — use whichever your course uses.

## Lesson 3 — Making a date

- **一緒に** (いっしょに) — together / razem  
  Lesson placement: Genki may list 一緒に in a later lesson; added here because it's needed for ～ませんか invitations.

## Lesson 4 — The first date

- **椅子** (いす) — chair / krzesło  
  Often written in kana (いす) in textbooks and everyday text; 椅子 is the dictionary kanji form.

## Lesson 5 — A trip to Okinawa

- **十** (とお) — ten (things) / dziesięć (przedmiotów); dziesięć sztuk  
  とお is the correct native reading, but in shops people usually say 十個 (じゅっこ) instead — check the example sounds natural to you.

## Lesson 7 — Family picture

- **背** (せ) — height (of a person); stature / wzrost (człowieka)  
  Example 背は何センチですか is everyday/colloquial; the formal word for body height is 身長 (しんちょう).

## Lesson 10 — Winter vacation plans

- **同じ** (おなじ) — same / taki sam; ten sam  
  Irregular: 同じ modifies nouns directly without な (同じ大学), but takes だ/です like a na-adjective. Tagged na-adj for lack of a better type.
